1. Who we are

2. Our programmes and services

We offer CeMAP exam training and related career support, currently:

  • CeMAP Core — a fixed-length, live online group programme;
  • Complete Pathway — CeMAP Core plus Beyond CeMAP and a bonus career clarity session;
  • Beyond CeMAP — a live online group programme on the industry and what comes after qualification, billed hourly;
  • Bespoke Mortgage Case Support — one-to-one help preparing a live case, scoped and quoted before any work begins.

Current programmes and prices are shown on our Programmes page. Prices may change for future cohorts, but the price you're quoted at enrolment is the price you pay for that cohort.

Rockgate Academy is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or approved by the London Institute of Banking & Finance (LIBF). CeMAP® is a registered trademark of LIBF. Completing a Rockgate Academy programme does not itself award CeMAP or any LIBF status — you must register for and sit the CeMAP exam directly through LIBF. Any LIBF credential shown on our website or by our founder reflects a personal qualification, not an accreditation of Rockgate Academy's training.

3. Enrolment and payment

You enrol by submitting an enquiry and confirming your place once we've responded. A place on a programme is only reserved once payment has been received in full, unless we agree a different arrangement with you in writing.

Payment is taken securely via Monzo. We do not store your card or bank details ourselves. Programme places are limited, and we may need to offer you the next available cohort if the one you enquired about is full.

4. Cancellation and refunds

Policy default — please confirm this reflects what you actually want to offer. The terms below follow the standard UK approach for online course sales, but the exact cancellation window and refund amount are business decisions for you to set, not facts I could look up.

If you enrol online, you have a 14-day cooling-off period under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, during which you can cancel for a full refund. If your programme's first live session falls within that 14-day window and you want to secure your place, we'll ask you to confirm you understand that your cancellation right ends once that first session has taken place.

Outside the cooling-off period, fees for CeMAP Core, Complete Pathway, and Beyond CeMAP are non-refundable once the programme has started, as places are limited and reserved on your behalf. If you need to withdraw before the programme starts, contact us — we'll aim to offer a transfer to the next available cohort rather than a refund, at our discretion.

If we have to cancel or postpone a cohort (for example, too few learners to run it), you'll be offered a full refund or a place on the next cohort, whichever you prefer.

5. Course materials

Study materials, slides, recordings, and quizzes provided as part of a programme are for your own personal use while learning with us. You may not copy, resell, redistribute, or publicly share them, in part or in full, without our written permission.

Where a programme includes replay access, replays are available for the duration of that programme unless stated otherwise.

6. Live sessions and conduct

Live sessions are a shared learning space. We ask all learners to be respectful of the instructor and other participants, and not to record or share sessions without everyone's agreement. We may remove a participant from a live session, without refund, if their conduct is abusive or disruptive to other learners.

7. Bespoke mortgage case support

Case support is scoped and quoted before any work begins, based on the information you give us about the case. The quoted fee is payable in full before work starts, unless we agree otherwise in writing.

This service supports your understanding and preparation of a case. It does not replace, override, or substitute your firm's CAS (Certificate/Competent Adviser Status) sign-off process — every case must still be reviewed and confirmed by your own CAS supervisor before submission.

8. Liability

We provide our programmes and case support with reasonable care and skill. Nothing in these terms limits or excludes our liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, or for fraud.

Beyond that, our total liability to you in connection with a programme or case support is limited to the amount you paid for it, and we are not liable for indirect or consequential losses, such as loss of income or lost career opportunities.

9. Complaints

If you're unhappy with a programme or with case support, please contact us first at hello@rockgateacademy.co.uk so we can try to put things right.

Rockgate Academy is a training provider, not a regulated financial services firm, so service complaints are not eligible for referral to the Financial Ombudsman Service — that route applies to regulated financial advice, not training.
