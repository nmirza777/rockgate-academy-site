Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

Do I need previous financial services experience? +

No. The consultation is designed for people considering the profession, including complete beginners.

Is this a recruitment service? +

No. This is independent career guidance, not a job placement or guaranteed employment service.

Will CeMAP mean I can immediately give regulated mortgage advice? +

No. Passing CeMAP alone does not grant CAS (Certificate/Competent Adviser Status) or authority to advise independently; employer, supervision and regulatory requirements also apply.

Is this a sales call? +

No. The fee pays for a substantive, personalised career discussion; any further training afterwards is entirely optional.

Can I reschedule or cancel? +

Yes — see our booking, rescheduling and refund terms below, which don't limit your statutory consumer rights.

Booking terms

Booking, rescheduling and refund terms

Your Statutory Cancellation Rights

Under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013, customers booking consultations online normally have the right to cancel within 14 days of entering into the contract, without giving a reason.

If you wish your consultation to take place during this 14-day period, we will ask you to expressly request that the service begins before the cancellation period expires.

If you cancel after the service has begun but before it has been fully delivered, you may be required to pay a proportionate amount for the services already provided, where permitted by law.

Once the consultation has been fully delivered, your statutory cancellation right may end, provided the necessary legal requirements have been satisfied, including your prior express request and acknowledgement that you will lose that right once the service is fully performed.

Our standard rescheduling and refund policies apply in addition to, and do not override, your statutory consumer rights.

Cancelling, rescheduling and refunds

If you need to cancel or reschedule before your consultation takes place, tell us by emailing hello@rockgateacademy.co.uk or messaging us on WhatsApp — we'll refund the full £50 fee, or move you to a new time, whichever you prefer. There is no minimum notice period for this. We'd simply appreciate as much notice as you can give, so the slot can be offered to someone else.

If you book a specific time and then don't attend without telling us beforehand, we won't have been able to deliver the session or offer that slot to anyone else. In that situation the fee for that missed appointment isn't refunded, but we will always offer you one free reschedule to a new time rather than requiring a new payment.
